# Segregate even and odd Elements in a Linked List

**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Link:** [https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/segregate-even-and-odd-elements-in-a-linked-list/](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/segregate-even-and-odd-elements-in-a-linked-list/)

---

## Problem Statement

```markdown
### Title:
Separate Even and Odd Numbers in Linked List

### Description:
Given the head of a linked list, separate even numbers from odd numbers. Even numbers should be moved to the front of the linked list and odd numbers should be moved to the end.

### Examples:
1. Input: 17->15->8->9->2->4->6
   Output: 8->2->4->6->15->9->17

2. Input: 10->12->14->16->18->20
   Output: 10->12->14->16->18->20

3. Input: 1->3->5->7->9
   Output: 1->3->5->7->9

### Constraints:
- The linked list is non-empty and contains at least one node.
- The nodes in the linked list contain non-negative integers.
- The maximum number of nodes in the linked list is 10^5.
```
