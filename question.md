## Problem Statement

**Title:** Segregate Evens and Odds in a Linked List

**Description:** Given the head of a singly linked list, rearrange the list in place so that all nodes containing even values appear before nodes containing odd values. Return the head of the modified linked list.

**Examples:**

1. Input: `8 -> 2 -> 4 -> 6 -> 17 -> 15 -> 9`
Output: `8 -> 2 -> 4 -> 6 -> 17 -> 15 -> 9`
Explanation: Since 8, 2, 4, and 6 are even, they appear first in the list, followed by the odd numbers 17, 15, and 9.

2. Input: `1 -> 3 -> 5 -> 7`
Output: `1 -> 3 -> 5 -> 7`
Explanation: There is no even number, so no modification is needed.

**Constraints:** The linked list is a singly linked list, and the nodes contain integers. The input list is not empty, and the numbers in the list are between 1 and 1000.

**Constraints:** 

The linked list has at most 100000 nodes, and the numbers in the list are between 1 and 1000000.
