## Problem Statement

**Title:** Linked List: Even-Odd Separator

**Description:** 
Given the head of a singly linked list, separate the list into two lists: one containing even numbers and the other containing odd numbers. The even numbers should be placed at the end of the result list.

**Examples:**

1. **Input:** Head of the linked list: `17 -> 15 -> 8 -> 9 -> 2 -> 4 -> 6`
   **Output:** `17->15->9->6->4->2`

2. **Input:** Head of the linked list: `2 -> 4 -> 6 -> 8 -> 10`
   **Output:** `2->4->6->8->10`

3. **Input:** Head of the linked list: `1 -> 3 -> 5 -> 7 -> 9`
   **Output:** `1->3->5->7->9`

**Constraints:**
- The linked list may contain negative numbers.
- The linked list may contain zero.
- The linked list may be empty.
- The linked list may contain a large number of nodes.
- The linked list may contain nodes with large values (up to 10^9).
- The linked list may contain nodes with small values (down to -10^9).
