## Problem Statement

Title: 
Fibonacci Number Sum


Description: 
Given two positive integers $m$ and $n$, calculate the sum of the $m^{th}$ and $n^{th}$ Fibonacci numbers.


Examples:

|  m | n  | result |
|----|----|--------|
| 1  | 2  | 3      |
| 5  | 7  | 144    |
| 10 | 15 | 610   |


Constraints:
$1 \leq m, n \leq 100$
